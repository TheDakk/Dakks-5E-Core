# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-03)

The whole 2014 SRD, every document with its own painting. Nine new compendiums beside Creatures, Spells and Items: Races, Classes, Subclasses, Class Features and Backgrounds (in a Classes & Origins folder), and Monster Features, Trade Goods, Tables and Rules (in a Reference folder). Every item now carries its own Dakk's painting (872 of 872: each +1, +2 and +3 weapon and armour, adamantine and mithral, armor of resistance, the named magic weapons on every base, every potion, ring, wand and wondrous item), as does every spell (319 of 319) and every creature (331 of 331), and every race, class, subclass, feature, trade good and table but three features the art library has still to paint (Defensive Tactics, Create Specter, Fire Breath). Stats are the system's SRD documents unchanged. Requires Dakk's Ultimate Tokens 3.7.0 or later.

## 1.1.0 (2026-09-29)

Both sexes on the table, sensible dispositions, and the system's latest SRD. Where Dakk's Ultimate Tokens paints a woman beside a creature, the pack now holds her as her own creature with the same stats, "Ogre (female)" beside "Ogre", each with its own portrait and token, so the GM places the one they want: 54 new actors (the giants, orcs, goblins, gnolls, lycanthropes, drow, the humanoid NPCs and more), each in its base creature's folder. Every creature's starting disposition was reviewed against its lore: 97 that the system marks hostile now start neutral (harmless and domestic animals, townsfolk and ordinary NPCs, the metallic dragons, celestials and other good creatures that would not attack on sight); the GM can still change any token. Eight more actors carry Dakk's art: the Thug, and the spell-effect actors (Arcane Eye, Arcane Hand, Arcane Sword, Dancing Lights, Mage Hand, Unseen Servant) now show their spell's painting. Rebuilt from the D&D 5e system 5.3.3, which brings the system's own fixes to 63 monsters and 74 spells (activities, targeting and descriptions); stats are otherwise the system's. Requires the D&D 5e system 5.3.0 or later and Dakk's Ultimate Tokens 3.5.0 or later (the women's paintings).

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-07)

First release. The D&D 5e system's 2014 SRD compendia (Monsters, Spells, Items; SRD 5.1, built from dnd5e 5.2.5 so that any 5.2.5 or later system loads it) rebuilt document for document with Dakk's Ultimate Tokens as every portrait, token and icon: 321 of 331 monsters, 188 of 319 spells and 53 of 872 items carry Dakk's art; the rest keep the system's own. Stats are untouched. Requires the D&D 5e system 5.2.5 or later (verified on 5.3.3) and the Dakk's Ultimate Tokens module 2.0.0 or later, which Foundry offers to install alongside; if the module unchecks itself when enabled, one of those two is below its minimum. The 2024 rules will be a separate module. Compendium: the three packs (Creatures, Spells, Items) sit under "Dakk's Ultimate Tokens" > "D&D 5e (2014)" in the sidebar, the same tree Dakk's D&D 2e Core uses, and carry banners painted in Dakk's hand, shared with the token module (2.3.3 or later). The module also carries a cover and icon for Foundry's setup and install screens. Version numbering begins at 1.0.0; the same-day 0.1.x builds were pre-release iterations of this release.
